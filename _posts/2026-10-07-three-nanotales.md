**I**

Darkness and fear. Doomsday so near.  
Bloody this snare. No escaping in here.

**II**

The Empire fell.[^footnote] Darkness everywhere.  
The Horsemen wail. Oh, what a despair!

**III**

The boy failed. He failed again.  
Only this time, he lost nothing.

&mdash; *The Outsider*  
*Written between 2016–2017.*

[^footnote]: I assure you, no empires were harmed in the process.

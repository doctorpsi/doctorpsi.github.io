Time is constantly beating fast  
To beat me, to the last—  
The Hours’ [^horae] conspiring against me  
To keep me, oh, aghast!  

Lo, here comes the illness  
To reduce me to nothingness—  
The Hours’ conspiring against me  
To betray my willingness!  

Yet Time can never mar me,  
And snatch away the glee  
‘Cause God[^theology] guides me, oh, see—  
Through the Hours’ Conspiracy!  

&mdash; *The Outsider*  
*February 16, 2010 (right before 12th boards)*

[^horae]: Referring to the [Horae](https://www.worldhistory.org/Horae/), the Greek goddesses of time and the seasons.  
    Loosely inspired by Tennyson’s *Tithonus*, which we read back in school.

[^theology]: The theology here has long expired; the conspiracy has not.
